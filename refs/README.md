## Amara Spatial 10K — what is actually in it

`ZeroOneCreative/amara-spatial-10k` on Hugging Face. 10,071 AI-generated
metric-scale meshes, not gated, 21 webdataset shards under `meshes/`, with
`index.parquet` / `manifest.parquet` for lookup and CLIP embeddings for
retrieval.

**It will not give us hero assets.** The taxonomy is 60+ top categories and
they are overwhelmingly interiors and everyday objects — Bathroom, Bookstore,
Casino, Classroom, Coffee shop, Kitchen, Library, Office, Wine Cellar. The only
vehicle-adjacent entries are `Cars`, `Hatchback Cars`, `Vehicle HP` and
`Paris-Inspired Emergency Vehicles`, and nothing in the set is a combat vehicle,
a weapon or a character in this game's idiom.

**Where it IS worth pulling from**, matched against what this game still draws
with primitives:

| need | category |
|---|---|
| pit-lane and scrapyard clutter | Construction Site Props, Urban Props |
| barrels, crates, toolboxes | Crates, Toolboxes |
| roadside and verge dressing | Nature Props, Building Exterior Props |
| wreck and salvage dressing | Shipwreck and Salvage |
| grandstand and arena fittings | Gym and Sport Centre, Commercial |

So: use Amara for **set dressing at volume**, and generate the hero vehicles,
characters and weapons from the references in this pack. That split also matches
where each source is strong — Amara gives quantity and metric scale, the
reference sheets give art direction.

Retrieval is by CLIP embedding rather than by filename, which is the useful part
for us: query with a description of what the scene needs and take the nearest
meshes, instead of guessing at names.
