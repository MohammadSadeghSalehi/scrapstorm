## Audio

Everything under `/assets/audio` is original and generated by the scripts in
`scripts/`. No commercial recording, stem, melody or lyric is sampled, quoted or
imitated anywhere in this project. `public/assets/audio/` is gitignored like the
rest of the heavy assets — **the scripts and the prompts recorded below are the
committed artefact**, and a fresh checkout reproduces the audio by running them.

### Reproduce

```
node scripts/gen-vo.mjs       # announcer + rival VO   (skips existing files)
node scripts/gen-music.mjs    # music beds             (skips existing files)
node scripts/gen-vo.mjs --list
node scripts/gen-music.mjs --list   # prints every prompt verbatim
```

Both read `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` from `.env` (gitignored, never logged).

### Procedural — no asset at all

The majority of what the player hears is synthesised at runtime and has no file
behind it: engines (`src/game/audio/engineModel.ts`), tyres/road (`tyreModel.ts`),
explosions (`explosion.ts`), weapons (`weaponModel.ts`), metal scrape, panel
crumple and glass (`impactModel.ts`), the arena crowd (`crowd.ts`) and the
reverb impulse responses (`reverb.ts`). These vary per event by construction,
which is the point — a sample cannot.

### Music — original arena rock (`scripts/gen-music.mjs`)

Rendered with the ElevenLabs Music API. The prompts describe **sound** —
instrumentation, tempo, key, arrangement, production era, mix register — and
deliberately name no artist, band or song. Full text of every prompt lives in
`scripts/gen-music.mjs` and is printable with `--list`; it is also written to
`public/assets/audio/music/manifest.json` at render time. Summary:

| track | length | brief |
|---|---|---|
| `menu_anthem` | 45 s loop | stadium rock anthem, 126 BPM, A major, wordless gang-vocal hook |
| `garage_vibe` | 45 s loop | blues-rock groove, 92 BPM, E minor, dry small-room |
| `race_heat` | 60 s loop | hard rock bed, 142 BPM, D major, palm-muted, no solos |
| `race_intensity` | 60 s loop | arena rock, 148 BPM, D major, soaring lead, gang shouts |
| `final_lap` | 60 s loop | arena rock at full urgency, 152 BPM, D major |
| `victory` | 20 s one-shot | major-key fanfare, 130 BPM, definite ending |
| `defeat` | 20 s one-shot | slow minor outro, 88 BPM, feedback and decay |

Constraints written into every prompt, and why: instrumental with no lead vocal
(a lead vocal fights the announcer), "seamless loop, no fade in/out" (these are
looped by `AudioEngine.playMusic` and a fade is audible at the seam every lap),
an explicit BPM (the mixer crossfades between unaligned tracks, so keeping the
race beds within ~10 BPM makes the seams survivable), and an explicit guitar
register (the engine synth owns 60–400 Hz and the tyre bed 400 Hz–3 kHz).

### Voice-over (`scripts/gen-vo.mjs`)

Two casts, both ElevenLabs text-to-speech, `eleven_multilingual_v2`. All lines
are original writing.

- **Announcer** — voice `VR6AewLTigWG4xSOukaG`, stability 0.55 / style 0.35.
  Circuit PA. Mixed dry, plus a horn-array send that only opens inside the
  arena sector.
- **Rival** — voice `TxGEqnHWrfWFTfGW9XjX`, stability 0.38 / style 0.55.
  Another driver on the radio. Mixed through a band-limited transceiver stage
  (480 Hz – 2.9 kHz, driven), which is why it is read looser and closer.

Line ids and text are printable with `node scripts/gen-vo.mjs --list` and are
mirrored into `public/assets/audio/vo/manifest.json`.

### Sound effects

`public/assets/audio/sfx/*.mp3` are the original ElevenLabs one-shots from the
first audio pass. They are now used as *layers and fallbacks* rather than as the
primary source: weapons, explosions, impacts and scrapes are synthesised, and
the samples sit underneath the player's own actions for recorded grit.
