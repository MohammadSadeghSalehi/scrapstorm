# T05 — Cars sit on the road, not a plane at y = 0

The ribbon has crests and bowls. Wreck respawns, props, and vehicle pose must
use `getGroundHeight(x, z)`.

**Visible spec:** Wreck on a crest. You respawn on that crest, not inside the
asphalt or hovering a metre above a dip.

**Do not** write `y: 0` or `y: 0.55` as a world height for something that
follows the road.
